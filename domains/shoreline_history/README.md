# Domain 2 — Shoreline History

## Study area
Shanghumugham–Valiyathura–Poonthura coast, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Real shoreline extraction instructions remain in `data/shoreline_history/thiruvananthapuram/README.md` and require CoastSat plus Google Earth Engine authentication.

## Synthetic development dataset
`data/synthetic/shoreline_observations_2015_2025.json` contains 6 illustrative transects with one record per year from 2015–2025 (66 records). Negative displacement/rate indicates landward retreat; positive values indicate seaward advance. The public compatibility copy is `public/domains/shoreline_history/data/synthetic/shoreline_observations.json`.

**Synthetic only:** these values are not measured shoreline positions, CoastSat output, or evidence of actual erosion. Replace them with reviewed detections before scientific use.
