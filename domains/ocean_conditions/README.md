# Domain 5 — Ocean Conditions

## Study area
Arabian Sea nearshore off Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.

## Synthetic development dataset
`data/synthetic/ocean_conditions_monthly_2015_2025.json` contains 132 monthly summaries from 2015–2025, including mean and upper-tail significant wave height, peak period, direction, approximate deepwater wave power, illustrative tidal range, and estimated storm days. The public compatibility copy retains the legacy filename `public/domains/ocean_conditions/data/synthetic/ocean_conditions_6h.json`.

**Synthetic only:** these are monthly summaries, not 6-hourly measurements. Replace with quality-controlled INCOIS/ERA5/TPXO data and harmonize tide datum before scientific use.
