# Domain 4 — Rainfall & Weather

## Study area
Thiruvananthapuram coast, Kerala. The synthetic monthly rainfall pattern is higher during the southwest monsoon (June–September), with a secondary October–November wet period.

## Synthetic development dataset
`data/synthetic/rainfall_weather_monthly_2015_2025.json` contains 132 monthly summaries from 2015–2025, including monthly rainfall, mean daily rainfall, an illustrative daily peak, wind, temperature, and pressure. The 7/30-day rainfall fields are scaled monthly-mean proxies, not true rolling accumulations. The public compatibility copy retains the legacy filename `public/domains/rainfall_weather/data/synthetic/rainfall_weather_daily.json`.

**Synthetic only:** values are not calibrated to local station data and are not forecasts.
